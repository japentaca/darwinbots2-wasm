When a bot reproduces, the engine sets aside what it has in cells 976 to 990 and
hands it to the child one per cycle: 976 arrives in time for the child to read it
at [[.robage]] 1, 977 at `.robage` 2, and so on up to 990, which can be read at
`.robage` 15. The parent keeps its own values.

Each delivery has two conditions:

- **The birth tie has to still be there.** If the child breaks it (or loses it),
  whatever was left to deliver is lost.
- **The child's cell has to be 0.** If the child has already written something
  there, the delivery does not overwrite it.

Also, if the bot has a child while it is still receiving its own deferred
inheritance, the deliveries it had left are lost.

```adn
' Before reproducing, the parent notes its energy in 976
cond
*.robage 10 =
start
*.nrg 976 store
50 .repro store
stop

' On its second cycle, the child copies what it inherited into 51
cond
*.robage 1 =
start
*976 51 store
stop
```

The child ends up with its parent's energy in cell 51. The parent also runs the
second gene at its age 1, but at that moment its 976 is still 0.

Compared with the [[sysvars/mem-971-975|instant]] one, this inheritance is slow
and fragile, but it lets the child decide: it can reject a cell by writing to it
before it arrives, or break the tie to receive no more. More details in
[[adn/memoria#memoria-genetica]].
